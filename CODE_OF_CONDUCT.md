# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the NAMROS Community Edition project a
welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being precise about technical risks.
- Keep discussion focused on the project and the problem being solved.
- Give and receive feedback with care.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of project discussions.
- Any behavior that makes collaboration unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may moderate discussions, close issues, remove comments, or
restrict participation when needed to protect the project community.
